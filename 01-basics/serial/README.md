## What I learned

- Serial:communication between your Arduino board and your computer (or another serial device).
-9600 is called the baud rate, which means the communication speed.
-Serial.print() sends data to the serial monitor
-print() does NOT move to the next line
-Serial.println() prints new line after the text to be printed
-Close the serial monitor before pressing upload
